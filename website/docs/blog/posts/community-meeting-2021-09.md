The September meeting refined alert policies, counter terminology, and the roles of labels and annotations.

<!-- more -->

## Summary

1. **Alert policies**:
   The group reviewed distinct conditions for budget exhaustion, remaining budget, and average burn rate. The proposal also included alerts when a condition resolves. Participants discussed ways to avoid alerts for expected spikes. [Discussion at 1:01](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wB0tcuXF7PI&t=61s).

2. **Counter terminology**:
   Participants agreed to rename the `incremental` metric type to `counter` to make its meaning clearer. [Discussion at 10:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wB0tcuXF7PI&t=609s).

3. **Labels and annotations**:
   Labels were proposed for categorization and search, with annotations for implementation-specific information. The group wanted concrete examples before deciding whether both fields were necessary. [Discussion at 15:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wB0tcuXF7PI&t=918s).

## Action Items

1. Open a pull request to rename `incremental` to `counter`.
2. Review the updated alert policy proposal.
3. Link the labels and annotations proposals, collect examples, and continue the discussion in GitHub and Slack.
