The February meeting covered progress toward a stable specification and a prototype for evaluating OpenSLO definitions.

<!-- more -->

## Summary

1. **Specification and tooling**:
   The group welcomed additional maintainers and reviewed the proposal for a separate SLI kind. Participants highlighted the need to coordinate specification versions with oslo support. [Discussion at 3:18](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=699h5DI1WlQ&t=198s).

2. **Time windows**:
   Shorthand durations needed to account for calendar-aligned windows, start times, and time zones. The group discussed these requirements without settling the full design. [Discussion at 10:46](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=699h5DI1WlQ&t=646s).

3. **Evaluation prototype**:
   Marius demonstrated a Python prototype that read definitions, processed CSV data with pandas, and calculated results per subsystem and overall. Participants discussed using recorded data to compare budgeting and alerting methods. [Demo at 41:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=699h5DI1WlQ&t=2487s).

## Action Items

1. Allow another week for feedback on the separate SLI proposal.
2. Define how specification changes and oslo releases stay compatible.
3. Continue reviewing calendar-aligned durations and the stable release scope.
